VERSION NOTES

What changed in V1

  • Choose Robot voice or My own voice before creating a set.
  • Each voice choice opens a dedicated creation workspace.
  • Auto-advance detects a correct typed answer and moves on after 1.5 seconds.
  • The next prompt is played automatically when auto-advance is enabled.
  • The answer field stays mounted and focused between questions to help keep the iPad keyboard open.
  • Hands-free playback remains a separate setting.
  • All new controls, popups and feedback are translated into English, Dutch, French and Spanish.
